Introduction: The main aim of this research is to see and investigate where there is a gender hesitancy towards the influenza vaccine and why.


Independent variable: The independent variable is the variable that is being investigated. In this study, the independent variable has been operationalised by comparing the flu vaccination status of both genders males and females. An independent measures design will be used where male participants will form condition one and female participants will form condition two.

Dependent variable: The dependent variable is the factor that is measured in the study. In this investigation, the dependent variable has been carriead out by recording whether participants have received the flu vaccine or have not received it within the last 12 months.

Extraneous variables: extraneous variables are factors that could affect the results of the research and cause bias. They can include natural characteristics, environmental factors or research factors. The following are potential extraneous variables that would be controlled 


Environmental- Noise during the study is an environmental factor that could act as an extraneous variable. Background noise while participants complete the questionnaires could affect their answers for example if participants are distracted, they may not fully concentrate on the questions and could provide inaccurate answers. I would control this by asking participants to complete the questionnaires in a quiet location like a quite library.


Research- Poor questionnaire design is a research factor that is another extraneous variable. If questions are leading participants may feel they to give a particular answer rather than a real honest response. This could affect the accuracy of the results about flu vaccination and differences between male and female participants. I would address this by following guidance on designing questionnaires and carrying out a pilot study before collecting the main data.
